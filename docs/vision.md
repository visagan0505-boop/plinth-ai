# Plinth Vision

Plinth is an AI-native operational intelligence platform for structural engineering consultancies operating in New Zealand, Australia, and the UK. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected workflows, legacy ERPs, and tribal operational knowledge with a structured engineering-specific operational platform.

Plinth is not generic CRM or project management software. It is designed to become the operational system of record for engineering consultancies, supporting project delivery, staffing, profitability, document control, auditability, transmittals, engineering workflow records, and future predictive operational intelligence.
