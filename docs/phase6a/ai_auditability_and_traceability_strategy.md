# AI Auditability and Traceability Strategy

## The Audit Trail of AI Interventions
Just as financial ledgers require append-only tracking, AI interactions require structural traceability. Plinth treats AI reasoning as an auditable operational event.

## Audit Logging Requirements
When an AI generates an operational summary or a risk assessment, the system logs:
- The exact prompt generated by the orchestration layer.
- The specific deterministic data slices (DTOs) injected into the context window.
- The specific vector chunks (RAG) retrieved.
- The LLM version and temperature used.
- The raw output generated.

## Replayable AI Memory
Because the exact context window and inputs are audited, Plinth allows administrators to replay an AI decision. If an AI gave bad advice in March, an auditor in December can pull the logs and see exactly which misleading time entry note or data snapshot caused the hallucination.

## Attributable Source Linking
In the UI, every AI-generated paragraph must include hyperlinked citation tokens (e.g., `[Invoice 102]`, `[Job Phase: Consent]`). These citations are structurally guaranteed by the traceability logs to point to the exact UUIDs used during generation.
