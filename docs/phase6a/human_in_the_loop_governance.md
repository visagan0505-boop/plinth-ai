# Human-in-the-Loop Governance

## Absolute Operational Supremacy
Plinth is a human-centric operational tool. AI is an accelerator, not an autonomous agent. 

## Human Approval Workflows
Any AI-generated recommendation that alters financial, operational, or staffing reality must be routed through standard approval gates. 
- AI creates a Draft Invoice -> Human clicks 'Issue Invoice'.
- AI suggests a WIP Writeoff -> Director clicks 'Approve Adjustment'.
- AI flags a staff member for Burnout -> Manager acknowledges the alert.

## Deterministic Override Rules
Humans retain the absolute right to override AI intelligence. If an AI flags a job as `CRITICAL RISK` due to an anomalous time entry, but the human knows it was a strategic decision, they can mute the AI flag. The override is captured in the audit log, creating a feedback loop to improve future AI reasoning.

## Explainability Requirements
The Human-in-the-Loop governance demands that human operators are given enough deterministic evidence to make an informed decision. A human cannot be expected to "approve" a black-box recommendation. The AI must present the raw data (time entries, burn graphs) alongside its recommendation.
