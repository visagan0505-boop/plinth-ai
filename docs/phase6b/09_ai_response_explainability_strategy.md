# AI Response Explainability Strategy

## Retrieval Attribution Rendering
Every factual claim generated by the AI MUST include a hyperlink to the underlying data source. E.g., "The margin degraded heavily in week 3 [Source: Profitability Snapshot W3]." Clicking the link opens a side-panel displaying the raw read model data.

## Reasoning Trace Visibility
Advanced users can click a "Show Reasoning" toggle. This exposes the raw orchestration log, revealing exactly which vector chunks were injected into the prompt and which deterministic endpoints were called to construct the answer.

## Confidence Rendering
The overall confidence score of the retrieval payload heavily influences the UI. An AI response generated from highly volatile data is prefixed with a yellow warning: "Warning: Data volatility is high. Insights may be unreliable."

## Uncertainty Disclosure
The prompt specifically instructs the LLM: "If you are extrapolating or making an educated guess based on semantic notes, you must preface the sentence with 'It appears that...' or 'It is likely that...'."

## Source Lineage Rendering
For semantic claims (e.g., "The client keeps changing their mind"), the AI must surface the exact textual snippets from the time entry notes that led it to that conclusion. 

## Hallucination Warnings
A persistent, non-dismissible banner exists at the bottom of all AI interfaces: "AI-generated insights are analytical theories and may contain errors. Always verify against the authoritative operational ledger."
