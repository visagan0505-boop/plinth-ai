# Intelligence Strategy

Plinth is designed to evolve into an operational intelligence platform.

## Future Intelligence Areas
- Profitability forecasting
- Utilisation analytics
- Staffing predictions
- Resource heatmaps
- Revision analysis
- Delivery risk scoring
- Benchmarking
- Fee recovery analytics
- Operational forecasting

## Rules
- Preserve historical truth
- Prefer explicit relationships
- Avoid schema drift
- Support analytical workloads
- Separate transactional and analytical systems
