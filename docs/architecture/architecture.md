# Plinth Architecture

## Core Stack
- Next.js 15 App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Claude/OpenAI APIs

## Core Principles
- Schema-as-contract
- Multi-tenant from day one
- RLS on every business table
- Modular operational domains
- Intelligence-first modelling
- Temporal operational truth
- Future analytics separation
- Event-driven operational workflows

## Operational Direction
Plinth is designed around real engineering consultancy operations:
- producer statements
- transmittals
- revisions
- signings
- staffing
- profitability
- operational forecasting
- auditability
- document lineage

The platform prioritises structured operational data over generic PM abstractions.

## Temporal Truth
Historical operational truth matters.

The platform must support:
- historical staff rates
- profitability reconstruction
- operational snapshots
- future analytical replay
- immutable audit compatibility

## Analytics Philosophy
Transactional and analytical workloads are treated separately.

Future systems include:
- forecasting engines
- profitability analytics
- staffing intelligence
- benchmarking
- AI operational tooling
