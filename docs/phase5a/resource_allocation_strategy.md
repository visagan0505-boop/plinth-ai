# Resource Allocation Strategy

## Operational Staffing Visibility
Plinth provides intelligence on where staff effort is being applied (which jobs, which phases, which disciplines), allowing management to visualize operational deployment.

## Allocation Pressure Metrics
By analyzing historical time entry frequency and utilisation rates on active jobs, the intelligence layer can identify structural bottlenecks (e.g., "The structural engineering discipline is currently running at 110% capacity across 5 active jobs").

## Future Capacity Modelling
The intelligence layer uses current burn rates to forecast when jobs will conclude their active phases, thereby modelling when staff will become available for new allocation.

## Scheduling Avoidance Boundaries
Plinth explicitly avoids becoming a microscopic task-scheduling tool (like Gantt chart software). We do not schedule a specific hour of work on a specific day. We analyze macro-level operational resource pressure to inform strategic leadership decisions.
