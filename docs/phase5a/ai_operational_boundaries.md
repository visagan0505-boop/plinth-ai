# AI Operational Boundaries

## AI MUST NEVER BE THE SOURCE OF OPERATIONAL TRUTH
The absolute highest rule of Plinth's architecture: AI systems are barred from acting as the source of truth for financial or operational data. Truth is strictly transactional, append-only, and deterministic.

## Where AI is Allowed
AI (LLMs, ML models, anomaly detectors) is explicitly permitted to:
- Analyze historical trends.
- Forecast future fee burn based on deterministic actuals.
- Summarize operational risk based on time entry descriptions.
- Detect anomalies in billing patterns.
- Recommend operational interventions to management.

## Where Deterministic Systems Remain Authoritative
- Invoice generation and numbering.
- Profitability calculation.
- WIP accumulation.
- User authentication and tenancy boundaries.
- Timesheet approval processing.

## Forbidden Autonomous Mutations
AI is strictly forbidden from autonomously executing destructive or financial mutations. An AI may *draft* an invoice or *suggest* a time entry adjustment, but a human operator MUST explicitly approve and execute the transaction via standard DTO-validated workflows.

## Auditability and Explainability
Any insight generated by an AI must be traceable back to the deterministic data it analyzed. "Black box" AI claims (e.g., "This job will lose money") must be accompanied by the deterministic data supporting the claim (e.g., "Because WIP velocity has exceeded 110% of baseline for 3 consecutive weeks").
