# Domain Glossary

## Core Operational Terms
- Job: Engineering project or engagement
- Phase: Major delivery stage
- Scope: Defined work package
- Component: Specific engineering element
- Discipline: Engineering service category
- Risk Tier: Operational/commercial risk classification
- Client: Organisation commissioning engineering services
- Office Location: Operational office associated with staff and jobs

## Schema Terms
- Job Number: Unique project identifier in YYYY-NNNN format
- Bootstrap Tenant: Temporary single-tenant development UUID
- Rate Period: Historical billing/cost rate band
- Billing Multiplier: bill_rate / cost_rate ratio
- Issue Number: Sequential document identifier per job/type
- Revision Letter: Sequential document revision suffix

## Engineering Terms
- Producer Statement: Legally significant engineering statement
- PS1: Producer Statement Design
- PS3: Producer Statement Construction
- PS4: Producer Statement Construction Review
- IFC: Issued For Construction
- RFI: Request For Information
- Peer Review: Independent engineering review
- Construction Monitoring: Site observation process
- Consent: Regulatory approval workflow
- Transmittal: Formal document dispatch record
- Signing: Digital signature workflow for engineering records

## Document Terms
- Document Stage: Project lifecycle stage applicable to a document
- Document Status: Approval workflow state of a document
- Document Lineage: Revision and issuance history chain
- Tag: Reusable tenant-scoped filtering label

## Commercial Terms
- Fee Proposal: Commercial scope proposal
- Variation: Approved scope or fee change
- Scope Creep: Uncontrolled work expansion
- Fee Recovery: Recoverable billable percentage
- Utilisation: Productive or billable time ratio
- Burn Rate: Budget consumption velocity
- Historical Profitability: Profitability calculated using historical rates

## Intelligence Terms
- Operational Intelligence: Structured operational analytics and forecasting
- Resource Allocation: Staff assignment planning
- Benchmarking: Operational comparison metrics
- Delivery Risk: Likelihood of operational overrun
- Domain Event: Immutable operational state-change signal
- Activity Feed: Chronological operational event stream
