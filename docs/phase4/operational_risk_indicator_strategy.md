# Operational Risk Indicator Strategy

This document defines the systemic triggers that highlight operational risk to Project Managers.

## 1. Objective Risk Derivation
Risk indicators must be derived from intersecting facts, not subjective human input.

## 2. Permitted Risk Indicators
- **Unissued High-Churn Deliverables**: A deliverable with > 5 Draft revisions that has never been Transmitted (indicates a stuck engineering process).
- **Orphaned Telemetry**: Time being logged against a Phase that has no Deliverables defined (indicates unstructured work or scope creep).
- **Post-Issuance Burn**: Significant time logged against a Scope *after* the final "For Construction" transmittal has been issued (indicates unresolved site queries or unbilled variation work).

## 3. Presentation
Risk indicators are presented as factual flags on the Dashboard. They do not trigger alarms, send emails, or halt workflows. They simply surface the operational intersection to the professional for decision-making.
